FIFA World Cup Tournament · 1970–2022

Half a century of matches, asking what actually decides them.

A machine-learning-ready dataset of 1,322 Men’s FIFA World Cup matches from 1970–2022, designed for predicting match outcomes such as win, draw, or loss. Unlike standard match logs, it includes 70 curated columns with advanced pre-match and per-half features engineered specifically for football predictive modeling.

Methodology

Source

1,322 team-match records across 14 tournaments, 1970–2022, with 70 engineered columns. View the dataset.

How to read it

Every match appears twice — once from each team’s point of view. A “team-match” is one of those rows, so all rates are shares of team-matches, not of fixtures.

Coverage

Possession, shots, passes and the per-half splits are recorded only for 2002–2022 (768 team-matches). Goals, results, cards, home/host and prior-form features span all 14 editions.

Definitions

Form bands use each team’s prior win rate going into a match, limited to teams with five or more previous matches. “Host” is the tournament’s host nation; “home” is the match’s nominal home side.

Caveats

Figures are rounded to whole percentages, so segments may not total exactly 100. Small samples — the 70%+ possession band (n=21), four-plus first-half shots on target (n=93) — carry wider uncertainty.

Build

Charts are hand-built SVG with no charting libraries; all figures are computed directly from the source rows. Relationships shown are associations, not proven cause and effect.
